Four training stages · one method

A date. Its weekday.

Learn to work out a weekday in your head. Start with your birthday, discover the calendar’s patterns and practise one step at a time.

12 December 1981 was a Saturday. Two weeks later, 26 December was also a Saturday. So 25 December was a Friday. That is the idea: start from a known date and count the days.
